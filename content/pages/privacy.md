## Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: October 4, 2025

### Introduction

Deploy/this ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website deploythis.co.

### Information We Collect

#### Analytics Data

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. This includes:

- Pages you visit
- Time spent on the site
- Browser type and version
- Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
- Geographic location (country/city level)
- Referral source

Google Analytics is disabled by default and is enabled only after you select **Accept** in the cookie consent banner. If you select **Decline**, analytics remains disabled. We have configured Google Analytics with:

- IP anonymization enabled
- Do Not Track (DNT) browser signals respected
- Session-only analytics cookie behavior where supported

#### Cookies

This website uses the following types of cookies:

**Essential Cookies**: Required for the website to function properly (cookie consent preference)

**Analytics Cookies**: Used to understand how you use the website (Google Analytics). These are used only after you accept analytics cookies.

You can manage your cookie preferences through the cookie consent banner that appears on your first visit.

### How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

- Improve website content and user experience
- Understand visitor behavior and preferences
- Optimize website performance
- Generate anonymous usage statistics

### Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

Analytics data is processed by Google Analytics in accordance with their privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Decline analytics cookies through our cookie consent banner
- Request information about data we have collected
- Request deletion of your data
- Opt out of Google Analytics using browser extensions

### GDPR Compliance

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- Right to access your personal data
- Right to rectification of inaccurate data
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing

To exercise these rights, please contact us at victor@deploythis.co
